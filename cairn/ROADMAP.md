- ~~**`axes_scaling_factor()` computes a factor from a `+Inf` fitted diagonal instead of refusing** (`R/axes_scaled_fit.R:116`)~~ **graduated 2026-08-04 → M71** (AC1/T1–T3): the guard tests `<= 0`, which `+Inf` fails, and `cov2cor()` then maps that row/column to zeros with a unit diagonal rather than to `NaN`, so `solve()` succeeds and `all(is.finite(si))` is `TRUE` — a scaling factor is computed from a corrupted matrix and reported as a corrected statistic, the one failure D-036/M68-D1 name as undetectable by the user. Not a hotfix on the same reasoning the sibling row carried: `axes_scaling_factor()` is unexported and no converged lavaan fit is known to produce an infinite fitted diagonal. Promote into whichever milestone next opens `R/axes_scaled_fit.R`, or on any evidence a real fit can reach it.
- **`axes_scaling_factor()` scales silently at a huge-but-finite fitted diagonal** (`R/axes_scaled_fit.R:145`): measured at the M71 review (F5→F1, scored 15 as out of M71's scope, reproduced live at the gate), `sigma[4, 4] <- 1e10` returns `scale = 0.9579018` with `reason = NULL` and no warning, while `axes_corrected_se()` on the same Σ̂ refuses with `"unidentified"` — so one surface reports NA SEs and the other reports scaled fit statistics from the same degenerate matrix. M71 refused the `+Inf` endpoint of that continuum, which the row it graduated conceded "no converged lavaan fit is known to produce"; the finite neighbourhood is the reachable part and is unguarded. Deciding it needs a *criterion* for degeneracy, not another point guard — likely a conditioning or magnitude test on the implied correlation matrix, and possibly its own oracle.
