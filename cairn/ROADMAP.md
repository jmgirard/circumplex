- Small-sample correction for trajectory draws: **A small-sample correction that enters `ssm_trajectory()` as `draws =`** (M155 review F1 and F2). The `coef`/`vcov` intervals condition on the estimated variance components, and a percentile parametric bootstrap of the fixed effects does not correct this: on complete balanced data the REML fixed effects equal OLS, so the replicates are normal-approximation draws (measured 2026-09-24, bootstrap SD at 0.95 to 1.03 of the Wald SE). The correction is a t-scaling of coefficient draws under a Satterthwaite or Kenward-Roger style df; nlme's own DF is 2095 for every coefficient here, so no rule for the between-person intercepts. Promote only with its own coverage oracle at small N (the M27 oracle ran 200 persons per cell); the growth vignette's Section 7 states the gap until then.
