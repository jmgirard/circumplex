- Fit-index guidance in `vignettes/evaluating-circumplex-structure.Rmd:92-96` omits two caveats its own sources state (surfaced while authoring the M41 source notes, 2026-07-19; no overlap swept in candidates or the archive). Hu & Bentler (1999) report that ML-based TLI, Mc, and RMSEA **overreject true-population models at small sample size**, and circumplex work often runs at modest n — `zimmermann2017.md` puts several SSM accuracy thresholds in the n = 50–200 range — yet the vignette offers the .95 CFI/TLI benchmark unqualified. Browne & Cudeck likewise call their own cutoffs "based on subjective judgment… cannot be regarded as infallible or correct", which the vignette does not carry. Both caveats are now recorded with anchors on `cairn/references/hu1999.md` and `browne1992a.md`, so a milestone taking this up has its sources ready. M41 deliberately changed no package file.
