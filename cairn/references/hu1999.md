# hu1999 — the SRMR and CFI/TLI cutoffs the structure note reports against

**Provenance.** Ingested 2026-07-19 by M41 from
`cairn/references/sources/hu1999.pdf` (gitignored).
Pagination: the journal's own, *SEM* 6(1) 1–55; the shelf PDF's page *n* is
printed page *n* (confirmed at PDF p. 28, whose running head reads 28), so the
abstract below is printed p. 1.
Extraction: verified 2026-07-19 against the source by two independent channels — `pdftotext -layout` and a visual read of the `pdftoppm`-rendered page image — covering every value on this page, all of which come from the p. 1 abstract; per M41-D1 the second channel is the implementing session's own read of the page image, which is independent of the text layer but is **not** a human attestation, and no value here has been read by a human — observed 2026-07-19.

**Citation.** Hu, L., & Bentler, P. M. (1999). Cutoff criteria for fit indexes
in covariance structure analysis: Conventional criteria versus new
alternatives. *Structural Equation Modeling, 6*(1), 1–55.

**Role.** The source of the SRMR threshold the package hard-codes
(`ssm_ci_srmr_good <- 0.08`) and of the CFI/TLI guidance the vignette gives
for reading `cpm_fit()`'s summary, which reports χ², RMSEA, SRMR, CFI, and TLI
(`R/cpm_fit.R:1407`, `R/cpm_oop.R:44,187-188`). Like the RMSEA cutoffs in
[browne1992a.md](browne1992a.md), these gate wording only, never estimation.

**Read this page together with `browne1992a.md`.** The two are cited as a pair
everywhere in the repo, and the division of labour is easy to get wrong — see
"What this source does *not* license" below.

## Extracted values

### The cutoff recommendations — abstract, p. 1, verbatim

> The results suggest that, for the ML method, a cutoff value close to .95 for
> TLI, BL89, CFI, RNI, and Gamma Hat; a cutoff value close to .90 for Mc; a
> cutoff value close to .08 for SRMR; and a cutoff value close to .06 for
> RMSEA are needed before we can conclude that there is a relatively good fit
> between the hypothesized model and the observed data.

| Index | Cutoff as printed |
|---|---|
| TLI, BL89, CFI, RNI, Gamma Hat | close to **.95** |
| Mc | close to **.90** |
| SRMR | close to **.08** |
| RMSEA | close to **.06** |

Three qualifiers are part of the recommendation and travel with the numbers:
every value is **"close to"** rather than exact; all are stated **"for the ML
method"**; and they mark **"a relatively good fit"**, not a verdict of
adequacy.

### The two-index strategy is the paper's headline, not the cutoffs alone

> Furthermore, the 2-index presentation strategy is required to reject
> reasonable proportions of various types of true-population and misspecified
> models.

The strategy is defined in the same abstract as using the ML-based SRMR **and
supplementing it with** one of TLI, BL89, RNI, CFI, Gamma Hat, Mc, or RMSEA.
The cutoffs above were derived *within* that combinational frame; a single
index compared against one of them is not what the paper recommends.

### The small-sample caveat — abstract, p. 1

> Finally, using the proposed cutoff criteria, the ML-based TLI, Mc, and
> RMSEA tend to overreject true-population models at small sample size and
> thus are less preferable when sample size is small.

## What this source does *not* license

**The package's RMSEA thresholds do not come from here.** Hu and Bentler's own
RMSEA cutoff is **.06**. The package's `ssm_ci_rmsea_reasonable <- 0.08` and
`ssm_ci_rmsea_poor <- 0.10` come from [browne1992a.md](browne1992a.md), and the
repo attributes them there correctly — but a reader who meets this page first
could easily assume otherwise, since both pages are cited in the same
sentences. Nothing in the repo cites Hu & Bentler for an RMSEA threshold, and
nothing should without re-opening this question: .06 and .08 are different
recommendations from different papers, not variants of one convention.

The repo also takes no position on BL89, RNI, Gamma Hat, or Mc, none of which
`cpm_fit()` computes. Their cutoffs are recorded above for completeness of the
quoted sentence, and nothing traces to them.

## Open questions

- Every value here is extracted from the **abstract** (p. 1). The body's own
  statement of the same recommendations — the Conclusions section, around
  pp. 27–28 — has **not** been read, and neither has Table 2's rejection-rate
  evidence. The abstract is the authors' own summary and is quoted verbatim,
  so nothing here is second-hand; but a milestone needing the hedging the body
  attaches to these numbers must read it rather than treat this page as
  complete — observed 2026-07-19.
- No value on this page has been read by a human. Both channels are machine
  channels operating on the same PDF (M41-D1), so a defect in the scan itself
  — as opposed to the text layer — would not have been caught — observed
  2026-07-19.
- **The small-sample caveat is not reflected in the repo's guidance.**
  `vignettes/evaluating-circumplex-structure.Rmd:95-96` offers the .95 CFI/TLI
  benchmark without noting that this paper reports TLI overrejecting
  true-population models at small *n*, and circumplex applications are
  frequently run at modest sample sizes — `zimmermann2017.md` puts several SSM
  accuracy thresholds in the n = 50–200 range. Whether the vignette should
  carry the caveat is a documentation question this milestone does not decide
  (M41 changes no package file) — observed 2026-07-19.
