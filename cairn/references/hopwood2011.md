# hopwood2011 — the ISC and its normative descriptive statistics

**Provenance.** Ingested 2026-08-06 by M73 from
`cairn/references/sources/hopwood2011.pdf` (gitignored), 33 pages, printed
pp. 707–740. Pagination: the article's own page numbers. The PDF is
**born-digital** (`Creator: 3B2 Total Publishing System 8.07f/W`,
`Producer: PDFlib PLOP 2.0.0p6 (SunOS)/Acrobat Distiller 8.1.0 (Windows)`,
2474 text characters per page; one 400x324 image, not full-page), not an OCR
scan. The anchor matters here more than the container: **every shipped M and SD
is printed inside Figure 2 on p. 717**, laid out around a circle rather than in
a table. Those glyphs are real text objects and the text layer reads them, but
a figure is exactly the case M72's lesson names, so p. 717 was read in two
independent channels — the `pdftotext` text layer and a 300-dpi page-image
render — and the channels agree on all sixteen values and on which octant
segment each sits in.
Extraction: verified 2026-08-06 — every value below read in both channels, the
channels agreeing on every value; no value read by a second human channel
— observed 2026-08-06.

**Citation.** Hopwood, C. J., Ansell, E. B., Pincus, A. L., Wright, A. G. C.,
Lukowitsky, M. R., & Roche, M. J. (2011). The circumplex structure of
interpersonal sensitivities. *Journal of Personality, 79*(4), 707–740.
DOI 10.1111/j.1467-6494.2011.00696.x.

**Role.** The published source for every shipped ISC norm value.

**On the sample.** Figure 2's own note reads "Descriptive values are from
Study 1". Study 1's total N is 1,336, randomly split into a derivation Sample 1
of n = 649 and a validation Sample 2 of n = 687 (pp. 714–715). The package
ships 649, the derivation half, which is the sample Figure 2's α, M and SD
describe.

**On the items and angles.** The article does not print the 64-item ISC or its
item-to-octant assignment — it describes an initial pool of 151 items and
quotes seven of them as examples (p. 714) — so the `Items` rows below are
recorded as not published. No assigned degree per octant is printed either.
