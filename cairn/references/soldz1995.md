# soldz1995 — the IIP-SC's originating paper and its outpatient norms (iipsc sample 2)

**Provenance.** Ingested 2026-08-07 by M74 from
`cairn/references/sources/soldz1995.pdf` (gitignored), 11 PDF pages carrying the
article's printed pp. 53–63. Pagination: the article's own page numbers.

This is the **first shelf source for which M42-D1's two-channel rule actually
fires**. The PDF is an **OCR scan** (`Producer: Adobe Acrobat Pro DC 15 Paper
Capture Plug-in`), so its text layer is one OCR engine's reading of the page
image and corroborates nothing about that image. The two channels used here are
therefore neither of them that text layer: a **direct read of a 200–300 dpi
page-image render**, and an **independent OCR pass over the same render with
`tesseract`**, a different engine from the one that produced the shelved text
layer. The shelved layer was also compared afterwards and agrees, but it is
recorded as a third opinion rather than counted as a witness.
Extraction: verified 2026-08-07 — Table 4 (p. 59), Table 2 (p. 57) and the
sample descriptions (p. 55) each read in those two channels, which agree on
every value, on every item's octant block, and on both printed sample sizes; no
value read by a second human channel — observed 2026-08-07.

**Citation.** Soldz, S., Budman, S., Demby, A., & Merry, J. (1995). A short form
of the Inventory of Interpersonal Problems Circumplex Scales.
*Assessment, 2*(1), 53–63. The masthead prints "ASSESSMENT / 1995, Volume 2,
Number 1, 53-63" with "Copyright © 1995 by Psychological Assessment Resources,
Inc."; no DOI appears anywhere in the article.

**Role.** The paper that constructed the IIP-SC, and the published source for
iipsc's **sample 2**, the outpatient norms. It also carries the instrument-level
rows for iipsc — the item-to-octant grouping (Table 2, p. 57), which
`hopwood2008` does not print, and the Angle rows, which no source prints.

**On the sample.** The shipped sample-2 values are the **Generic Outpatient
sample**, the third of the article's three clinical samples: patients seen in
nine outpatient clinics of Harvard Community Health Plan, mean age 32.1 years
(range 18–60), 93% White. The article states its size twice and differently —
the narrative on p. 55 says "The third sample was composed of 105 patients
(71 female, 34 male)", while Table 4's own column heading on p. 59 reads
"(n = 106)". The Size row below records **106**, the n of the table the norm
values are read from, matching what the package ships; the 105 is recorded here
and nowhere resolved by the article.

**On the angles.** No assigned degree per octant is printed. The article
reports angular *deviations* from expected locations for its structural
analyses (pp. 61–62), which are estimates and not a degree convention, so the
Angle rows below are recorded as not published (IP2).

**On the item text.** Not an audited field — the package ships only elided
fragments of the IIP-SC items, since the item text is not the maintainer's to
publish — but the fragments were compared against Table 2 while it was open and
all 32 sit in the block Table 2 puts them in. One is worth recording: the
package's FG fragment reads "...join in on...", which matches how `hopwood2008`
quotes the item (p. 615, "It is hard for me to join in on groups") rather than
how Table 2 prints it here ("It is hard for me to join in groups"). The two
papers genuinely differ on the word.
