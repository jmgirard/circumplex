# M147: The certificate is the sole conditioning judge

**Status:** done (2026-09-21, PR #182 https://github.com/jmgirard/circumplex/pull/182)

**Goal:** The per-fit accuracy certificate alone decides whether an `axes_reliability()` fit computes or refuses on conditioning, so the platform-dependent `"unidentified"` refusal that RR22 measured at counterexample B leaves the shipped package.

**Outcome:** `axes_pricing_core()` (R/axes_corrected_se.R) inverts the information matrix with `solve(info, tol = 0)` and returns `"unidentified"` on three exact grounds only: two bit-identical component matrices (`identical(num.eq = FALSE)`), a component matrix identical to `diag(p)`, or a non-finite or exactly singular inversion. It returns `rcond(info)` as data. `axes_degeneracy_refusal()` prices the core once per fit inside a `tryCatch` fence and routes the fit to `axes_accuracy_certificate()` when the floor fired or when `rcond(info)` is below `sqrt(.Machine$double.eps)`; a `"singular"` from the core past the floor falls through to the certificate only on the `"ill_conditioned"` branch. The refusal object carries the core to both surfaces and pins the matrix and the derivative set itself (`axes_derivs_pin()`), checked by `axes_check_shared_refusal()`. `axes_se_derivs()` strips names before `outer()`. The raw lavaan-tie arm keeps the default tolerance in `naive_reason`. `tests/testthat/test-axes-certificate.R` admits a refusal only as an exact zero pivot; the rcond band and `cert_refusal_admitted()` are gone. New `test-axes-conditioning-judge.R`. The sweep `devel/degeneracy-oracle/tol0_sweep.R` (532 matrices, six families, exact-rational oracle in the region) and its results are committed; its five clauses pass. DESIGN.md Known fragilities corrected to five. NEWS and `?axes_reliability` state the change and the one caveat: a floor-admitted fit with fewer than four scales, which the exported function refuses at its door, can now be refused `"uncertified"` at the internal helpers.

**Decisions:** D-062 (selector at `sqrt(eps)`, three exact grounds; supersedes D-061's threshold and two-grounds clauses). Milestone-local: the certificate is licensed below eps by mechanism (RR24 Q1); the duplicate check reads only the component matrices; the p = 3 Q4 row `t = 0.999975` is the first floor-admitted matrix past the target, out of domain. Plan-gate choices and their falsifiers are in the work log (git).
