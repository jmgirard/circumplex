# M160: Selection guards for items and the remaining column arguments

**Status:** done (2026-10-18, PR #195 https://github.com/jmgirard/circumplex/pull/195)

**Goal:** The column-selection arguments that M159 left unguarded refuse the selections that give a wrong result or a misleading error, with an error that names the cause.

**Outcome:** `check_scales_selection()` and `check_scales_positions()` in `R/utils.R` take `arg` and `where`, so they also judge `items` (M159 messages unchanged). `score()`, `ipsatize()` and `self_standardize()` call them after matrix conversion. `axes_reliability()` checks both `items` forms on the data and `cormat` paths through a new `axes_check_items()` in `axes_resolve_map()`: it also refuses a number reaching a repeated name (it reads items by name), one column under two scales, and judges a factor element as its labels. `ssm_analyze_long()` refuses numbers reaching two same-named columns before its reshape. `check_scales_labels()` refuses `NA` listed twice. A new `check_measures_not_scales()` refuses a `measures` entry carrying a scale's name in `ssm_sem()` and `ssm_sem_syntax()`. Refusals are errors at once, with no deprecation cycle (D-065). Tests in `tests/testthat/test-items-selection.R` and `test-scales-selection.R`, case tables in `tests/testthat/helper-selection.R`; the export-listing test fires a refusal through every export with `scales` or `items`. Nine help params and two NEWS "Breaking changes" bullets updated.

**Decisions:** D-065 (a repeat, a position below 1 or a duplicated name in a column selection is a wrong-object input, refused at once also where it gives a defined result today; the user's choice at the merge question). Implement chose name comparison for the measures check, and refusing a number that reaches the first of two same-named columns.
