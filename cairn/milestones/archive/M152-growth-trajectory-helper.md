# M152: Growth output helper, coefficients or draws to a trajectory

**Status:** done (2026-09-24, PR #187 https://github.com/jmgirard/circumplex/pull/187)

**Goal:** Ship `ssm_trajectory()`. It turns a fitted joint model's fixed effects and covariance, or its posterior draws, into a certified trajectory table that prints and plots.

**Outcome:** `ssm_trajectory(coef, vcov, times, draws, time, interval, n_draws, contrast)` in `R/ssm_trajectory_helper.R`. Shape one draws `n_draws` coefficient vectors once by `mvn_draws()`; shape two takes a draws matrix as given (`b_` prefix dropped, other columns ignored, no random number drawn). The default contrast reads `dv<e|x|y>` and `dv<e|x|y>:<time>`; a `contrast` function replaces it, its rows unnamed or named `e, x, y` in order. Each time's `(e, x, y)` draws go through `ssm_draws(type = "parameters")`, so the columns are that function's medians, circular mean and interval bounds, plus `certified`. Refusals: partial argument combinations, a non-symmetric, non-PSD or misnamed `vcov`, an infinite draw, a missing coefficient name, and a `vcov` whose implied `Cov(x(t), y(t))` is exactly zero at every time over the block the contrast uses (an all-zero block and the draws shape are exempt). The object is a data frame of class `circumplex_ssm_trajectory` with a `time` attribute; print rounds to `digits`, marks uncertified and `NA` rows with `*`, and states the small-sample caution unconditionally; `ssm_plot_trajectory()` gains a method reading the attribute. Tests: the pre-M153 vignette loop as the verbatim reference (1e-12 on both fixture fits), closed-form and absolute normal-theory oracles, seam waves decided by a delta-method interval, origin and flat cases, the four-position cross-covariance plant matrix, a brms-shaped matrix, print, `layer_data()` identity and one vdiffr snapshot. NEWS entry and the pkgdown "Growth Model Functions" row.

**Decisions:** none milestone-local. Print `digits` defaults to 2 and `n_draws` is unused and unchecked under `draws`, both recorded in the work log. D-064 (plan gate) governs the helper.
