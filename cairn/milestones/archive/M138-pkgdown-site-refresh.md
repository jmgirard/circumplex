# M138: The website has a light navbar, a theme switch and grouped vignette menus

**Status:** done (2026-09-17, PR #171 https://github.com/jmgirard/circumplex/pull/171)

**Goal:** The pkgdown site drops its dark navbar for a theme the maintainer picks, gains a light and dark mode switch, and groups its Vignettes menu by level.

**Outcome:** `_pkgdown.yml` moves from `template: params: bootswatch: flatly` to `template: bslib: preset: zephyr` and adds `template: light-switch: true`, so pkgdown's `data_navbar()` emits no navbar background class and no `data-bs-theme` attribute. The hand-written `navbar: right:` list becomes `navbar: structure: right: [search, lightswitch, github]`, which restores pkgdown's search box and theme switch; the dead docsearch keys go with it, including a 2018 Algolia key. The Vignettes menu keeps its hand-written `navbar: left:` form and gains a `text:`-only heading per level with `"---------"` dividers, in the articles index's order. `tools/check-pkgdown-vignettes.R` now skips dividers, reads each page under the heading above it, requires the menu's headings to be exactly the level map's in its order, and fails a page filed under the wrong heading.

**Decisions:** none milestone-local. The plan gate's three choices — a preset over hand-tuned bslib colors, pkgdown over an `altdoc` Quarto site, a guard repair over widening its promise — are in the milestone's work log, and the `altdoc` alternative has a ROADMAP candidate row.

**Review:** three-lens fan-out, 11 + 6 + 0 findings, none returning the milestone. AC1 took two amendment rounds before this pass: its first wording asserted `bg-dark` on master, its second quantified over every `<nav>` line and so was false of the 96 pages carrying a table-of-contents nav. One finding fixed now: the `_pkgdown.yml` heading comment now states `menu_type()`'s real order (`menu:`, then the dash pattern, then `theme:`, then heading). Five guard findings — divider count and placement unchecked, `under[[""]]` appending on an empty heading, contents unchecked for a group title outside `LEVELS`, a page above the first heading skipping its title check, and the dash-plus-href message — were dispositioned at the hygiene gate into a promoted guard-hardening milestone rather than extending a twice-absorbed candidate row. The Algolia key's survival in git history took its own candidate row. Three rejections: pkgdown's default component order, the guard running in no workflow (out per Scope), and a pre-existing `http://` url. One accepted limitation into DESIGN.md: AC1 tests that pkgdown wrote no navbar background class, not that the navbar renders light.
