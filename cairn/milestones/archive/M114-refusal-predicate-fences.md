# M114: Pin the shared refusal predicate, and assert the dead literal rather than dropping it

**Status:** done (2026-08-30, PR #145 https://github.com/jmgirard/circumplex/pull/145)

**Goal:** Fence the one-predicate-both-surfaces refusal design against the per-surface alternative it was chosen over, and assert the deadness claim that motivated dropping `"ill_conditioned"` from the nestedness set without paying the backstop coverage dropping it would cost.

**Outcome:** Tests only; no shipped behaviour moved. `axes_degeneracy_refusal()` gates both surfaces on `axes_certificate_worst()`, the max over the certificate's `se`/`cval`/`fiml_ratio`, and until now nothing forced that — every committed case had all three fields on one side of `axes_degeneracy_delta_star`. A straddling input is now committed: `m106_family_c(eps, xi1 = 0.1, xi2 = 0.3)` at eps 3e-9/5e-9/8e-9, p = 4, where `cval` runs 0.040–0.50 against a 1e-4 target while `se` and `fiml_ratio` sit 3–4 decades inside it. Both surfaces are asserted to refuse `"uncertified"` on each; a per-surface split (`max(se, fiml_ratio)` at the SE helper, `cval` at the scaling surface) reddens the SE helper across the band. A second test drives the other two fields from a stubbed certificate with a both-directions liveness check. T1's search — 9,588 candidates, 6,666 reaching the certificate, 388 straddling — found families A and B produce none at any parameter tried: their three fields climb together and both pricing routes fail at once. On the AC8 grid, `check_nested()` keeps `"ill_conditioned"` in its guard set (`axes_scaling_factor()`'s `cval <= 0` backstop still emits it) and now asserts that neither surface's `reason` returns it, on all 34 inflation matrices per map plus the indefinite and near-singular cases across three maps.

**Decisions:** none cross-cutting. Milestone-local, at the plan gate: keep the literal and assert its deadness rather than dropping it as M111 review F12 proposed, since dropping it would silently retire nestedness coverage of the live backstop; falsified by that backstop being removed or proved unreachable.

**Review:** Three-lens fan-out (user-facing tier). Both Sonnet lenses reported no findings — the new AC8 assertions are strictly additive and strictly stronger than the untouched nestedness check, and the diff complies with the M108-era lesson to fence a refusal warning's route rather than its digits. The Opus diff-bug lens raised ten. Seven were fixed at the gate on the maintainer's decision, all silent-vacuity hardening in the milestone's own new tests: a `format()` round-trip between the straddle test and the loop it feeds replaced by a logical mask (a widened band could have emptied the loop while the non-emptiness guard passed); `expect_named()` on `axes_certificate_sentinel()` so a fourth certificate field reddens rather than leaving the hand-written stubs short; both case lists' lengths asserted; the warning-note text derived from the target constant rather than spelled `"0.01"`; the df pair taken from `m111_dfs()`; one warning per refusal asserted in the stub test; and one misleading comment corrected. Three rejected: bounding the straddle band to an expected set (deliberate platform tolerance, and bounding it re-imports the M113 windows problem), two stale source-line citations (pre-existing, in plan-owned text), and an observation that a later correction to the `cval` estimate may collapse the straddle — loudly, thanks to the non-emptiness guard. Nothing retired or graduated. `devtools::check(args = "--no-manual")` Status OK 0/0/0; all five CI checks green including windows-latest.
