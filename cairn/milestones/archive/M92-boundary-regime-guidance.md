# M92: Teach the boundary regime the structure vignette's own example is in

**Status:** done (2026-08-17, PR #120 https://github.com/jmgirard/circumplex/pull/120)

**Goal:** Teach a reader whose CPM fit prints a boundary or weak-identification note what it means for each reported number and what to do next, on the fit the structure vignette already displays.

**Outcome:** `vignettes/evaluating-circumplex-structure.Rmd` gains `### When a fit sits at a boundary`, which reads the vignette's own displayed `jz2017` fit — NO at ζ̂ = 1.000 with a zero-width percentile interval, the printed Heywood note, the ill-conditioning warning — glosses all five labels `cpm_boundary_markers()` returns, demonstrates the printed fired-marker list on a `cpm_simulate()` draw at N = 2500 (where the marker list's N gate opens), states an interval consequence per parameter family scoped to the analytic path the record measured, names four things the record does not support, and closes with a locate/re-fit/report/keep-using ladder. `?summary.circumplex_cpm` cross-references it. The estimated-angles paragraph is corrected: the reference scale is named as fixed, the preserved cyclic ordering and the 18.8°–78.7° adjacent spacings are stated, and the retired "little practical impact on SSM profiles" claim is dropped rather than restated. `cpm_marker_labels()` (`R/cpm_fit.R`) now holds the label vocabulary that `cpm_boundary_markers()` indexes. `tests/testthat/test-cpm_boundary_vignette.R` guards the section by evaluating the vignette's own chunks: label catalog vs subsection text, the demo's fired set and the paragraph naming it, the displayed fit's Heywood marker and zero-width interval and ill-conditioning warning, the angle figures and ordering, and the help-page cross-reference (via `tools::Rd_db()` where `man/` is absent; skipped where the package installs without vignettes, as covr does).

**Decisions:** the claims ledger lives in the milestone-local Decisions section, the plan-owned body having stood at 128 of 150 lines. Validation-simulation figures stay out of the shipped prose (they do not ship, so a reader cannot trace them — the M77 precedent); direction and consequence are taught qualitatively and every concrete number comes from output printed on the page.
