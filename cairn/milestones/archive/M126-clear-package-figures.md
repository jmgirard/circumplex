# M126: Package figures show every point, interval and axis label clearly

**Status:** done (2026-09-15, PR #159 https://github.com/jmgirard/circumplex/pull/159)

**Goal:** `plot()` for CPM fits, `ssm_plot_circle()` and `ssm_plot_trajectory()` draw figures in which each interval, point estimate and axis label can be read at vignette size.

**Outcome:** `plot.circumplex_cpm()` draws a zero-width angle interval as a radial line, a zero-width communality interval as an arc, and a both-zero interval as a short cap centred on the interval, in a `geom_segment()` layer. `geom_ssm_arc()` is unchanged. A scale drawn without its interval is named in one warning with its reason: inestimable interval, full-circle angle interval, or communality interval at 0. The new internal `ssm_r_axis_angle_clear()` in `R/coord_circumplex.R` puts the amplitude axis at the midpoint of the widest spoke gap that holds no point estimate, with ties to the smallest midpoint. A point within 1e-4 degrees of a spoke counts as on it, so optimizer error cannot pick the gap. When every gap holds a point, `ssm_r_axis_angle()` applies. Both `ssm_plot_circle()` and CPM plots use it. The `ssm_plot_trajectory()` help page now states bound placement as `ssm_interval_on_branch()` computes it. The `occasions-plot` figure height went from 6 to 3.2. The `occasions-path` chunk builds its canvas from parts with `r_axis_angle = 67.5` and PANO labels. Four vignette PNGs and the `cpm_plot` and `cross-zero circle` snapshots changed, and NEWS has five entries.

**Decisions:** zero-width CPM intervals are drawn as lines and a fixed-length cap inside `plot.circumplex_cpm()` only (implement gate). AC1 was amended once to exclude a communality interval with upper bound 0, which maps to the canvas centre.

**Review:** fresh evidence for all five criteria. Tests had 0 failures with the same 9 warnings as master, check had 0 errors, warnings and notes, and all four figures were viewed. The three-lens fan-out found no blame-history or prior-review findings. The diff-bug lens found 12 and the prior-review lens found 1. The maintainer accepted fix-now for six: the axis tolerance for fitted angles, the cap and axis wording in help and NEWS, two stale advanced-visualization sentences, the reason-text test, and the cap-centring test. Seven were rejected as already true on master or matching package convention: exact-zero width, the Set2 palette limit, a no-angle scale, marks outside the rim, 0/360 storage, the hard-coded 67.5, and the hand-built canvas layers. Ubuntu CI then failed a test that bounded fitted-angle error at 1e-6 degrees, which holds on macOS only. The bound became the 1e-4 tolerance the code uses, and the merge went ahead on green CI with approval re-given. The PR conversation was empty. Local work-log commits after the last push (PR URL, conversation read, re-approval) did not reach master, and this summary holds them.
