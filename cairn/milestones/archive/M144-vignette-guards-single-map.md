# M144: The vignette guards keep one level map, check section numbers, and pkgdown builds the vignette menu

**Status:** done (2026-09-21, PR #178 https://github.com/jmgirard/circumplex/pull/178)

**Goal:** The vignette guards that remain each check shipped behavior from one level map, and the guard with no shipped job is gone.

**Outcome:** `frame_levels` and `frame_next` moved to `tests/testthat/helper-vignette-frame.R`. The frame test reads them there, and `tools/check-pkgdown-vignettes.R` gets them by `sys.source()` and derives the level-to-pages list by `split()` on the map. The headings test now requires `Section N, "Title"` in the Overview for every numbered heading. `_pkgdown.yml` gives each `articles:` group a `navbar:` heading and drops the hand-written Vignettes menu. `navbar: components:` defines home, instruments and a `news` entry kept labeled News, and `structure: left:` orders them, since a present `navbar: left:` beats `structure:`. The guard lost its menu-parsing half. It now diffs `vignettes/` and the `articles:` groups (order, contents, `navbar:` key) against the map, reports an unknown group's contents, and runs as a step in `.github/workflows/pkgdown.yaml` before the build. `tools/check-vignette-split.R` is deleted. The boundary test's scoping comment is reworded, and there is one NEWS entry. The delivered candidate row "Harden the vignette frame and split guards" is struck.

**Decisions:** milestone-local M144-D1: pkgdown builds the grouped menu, which supersedes the M138 plan's rule-out of `articles: navbar:`. The accepted costs are the Articles label and the leading divider, recorded in `cairn/DESIGN.md` accepted limitations. Plan-gate choices: delete the split guard (it can come back from git if a third split is planned), prune three frame-test false-failure shapes, and home the map in a testthat helper instead of reading `_pkgdown.yml`.
