# M127: Printed reports are shorter and easier to scan

**Status:** done (2026-09-15, PR #160 https://github.com/jmgirard/circumplex/pull/160)

**Goal:** The printed accuracy summary and the printed invariance ladder show their verdicts and cautions in a shorter layout, and no printed message uses a double hyphen as a dash.

**Outcome:** `summary.circumplex_ci_accuracy()` prints three settings sentences, the structure note, the verdict blocks and one table from the new internal `ssm_ci_summary_table()`. The table has one row per profile and condition, with columns e, x, y, a, d, d_cert, cert and Structural. The other coverage and guardrail columns stay only in the returned object. On the five probes, summary line counts at width 80 fell from 80, 79, 81, 146 and 134 to 35, 34, 36, 68 and 55. The ladder block of `print.circumplex_ssm_sem()` moved to the internal `sem_print_invariance()`. It wraps the notes, the ΔCFI note and the verdict to `getOption("width")`. A verdict that ends in a period gets no second one. Printed `--` dashes in `R/ssm_ci_oop.R`, `R/ssm_sem.R`, `R/axes_reliability_oop.R` and `R/axes_corrected_se.R` became colons, commas or periods. The test helper `expect_short_ci_summary()` checks the length limit. NEWS has three entries, and three precomputed vignettes were re-knitted.

**Decisions:** D-056 lets a printed report change layout and wording without a deprecation cycle. D-057 corrects it: a table column can stop printing when it stays in the returned object. Question gate: one merged table, and the nine tab-aligned setting lines became three prose lines.

**Review:** All four criteria had fresh evidence. For AC1, a one-off script compared old and new summaries from a `git archive` of `845fb5e7`, and all 130 old verdict and caution lines matched after dash-only normalization. Tests had 0 failures with the same 9 warnings as master, and check had 0 errors, warnings and notes. The three-lens fan-out gave 10 diff-bug findings, 2 blame-history findings and no prior-review evidence. Fixed at the gate: the rank-deficiency caution now names where the unprinted width and pass rate are, the heading says "liberal band" again, and the stored above-rung verdict says "not required for this contrast" again. The table keys rows on `coverage$Condition`, and a stale header comment was fixed. The O5 test gap became a candidate row.
