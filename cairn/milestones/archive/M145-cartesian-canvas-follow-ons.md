# M145: Per-side label margins and plotmath crosshair labels on the circumplex canvas

**Status:** done (2026-09-21, PR #179 https://github.com/jmgirard/circumplex/pull/179)

**Goal:** Give `ggcircumplex(angle_labels = TRUE)` a plot margin sized per side from the labels that point toward that side, and draw plotmath amplitude labels on the Cartesian crosshair as plotmath.

**Outcome:** The new internal `angle_label_margin()` in `R/ssm_plot.R` sets each `plot.margin` side to the larger of two values. One is `theme_circumplex()`'s margin. The other is the longest reach toward that side, `max(0, u) * 0.5 * font_size * nchar(label)`. The count includes the ` (θ°)` suffix. It reads the theme margin as a number after a points-unit check, so building the plot opens no graphics device. Both grids use the rule. The circle can sit off-center. In `R/coord_circumplex.R`, `cartesian_grid_grob()` keeps language labels as a list and draws them as an expression. `negate_axis_label()` puts a bare minus on a symbol, a non-negative number, or a `[`, `^` or `(` call, and puts parentheses around everything else. `is_blank_axis_label()` is the blank test for language objects. The work also updated roxygen, NEWS, the DESIGN.md canvas and grid-mode entries, and one vdiffr snapshot. The candidate row "Cartesian canvas follow-ons" (M142 review O4, O7, O12) is removed.

**Decisions:** Plan-gate choices: estimate label length rather than measure rendered widths, and accept off-center margins rather than a centered circle. The work log records the falsification conditions for both.
