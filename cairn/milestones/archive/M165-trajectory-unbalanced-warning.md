# M165: ssm_trajectory() warns on a vcov from an unbalanced fit

**Status:** done (2026-10-20, PR #199 https://github.com/jmgirard/circumplex/pull/199)

**Goal:** `ssm_trajectory()` warns when the structure of the `vcov` it is given shows that the joint growth model was not fit to complete balanced data, the design that its help page's small-sample coverage figures come from.

**Outcome:** For the `coef` and `vcov` shape with the default contrast, `ssm_trajectory()` reads, per coordinate e, x, y, `t_k = -cov(intercept, slope) / var(slope)` (the time of smallest fitted-value variance), located by position through `names(coef)`. It warns when `max(t_k) - min(t_k) > sqrt(.Machine$double.eps) * max(1, max(abs(t_k)))`, naming the three times and the spread. The check is skipped when a slope variance is not positive or a `t_k` is not finite, and for the `draws` shape and a `contrast` function. For the `ssm_growth_formula()` model on complete balanced data the three equal the mean design time for any variance components. Equal counts per person and coordinate-symmetric variance components also keep them equal, so silence does not show balance. Unequal counts, random slopes and other residual structures move them. Tests use a closed-form GLS covariance oracle, the glmmTMB fixture fits, and live nlme fits (REML, ML, time + 2000, 10% of person-waves removed). Measured balanced spreads were 1e-15 to 1.5e-10, and unbalanced ones 6.6e-4 and 1.7e-3. The help page now says the coverage oracle used complete balanced data at five waves. NEWS has one bullet. Growth vignette Section 7 adds one sentence, and Section 4 names "this joint-fit check".

**Decisions:** one milestone-local: GP4 does not bind a call-time warning that leaves signatures, the returned table and its print unchanged. The plan gate chose a call-time warning over a print-time caution, a relative threshold over an absolute one, and the default contrast only.
