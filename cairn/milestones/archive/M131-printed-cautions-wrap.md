# M131: Printed cautions and notes wrap to the reader's width

**Status:** done (2026-09-16, PR #164 https://github.com/jmgirard/circumplex/pull/164)

**Goal:** Every prose caution and note the package prints wraps to `getOption("width")`.

**Outcome:** `wrap_prose()` and `cat_prose()` in `R/utils.R` wrap prose to the console width, counting display columns (`disp_width()`), charging the prefix and continuation indent against the width, treating each element of `x` as its own paragraph, and keeping atomic units such as marker labels whole. `width` and `atomic` are validated with `stopifnot()`. The 41 census emitters in `R/cpm_oop.R`, `R/ssm_oop.R`, `R/ssm_draws.R`, `R/fit_structure_oop.R`, `R/axes_reliability_oop.R` and `R/ssm_ci_oop.R` (hand-wrapped, unwrapped, or wrapped at a hardcoded 70 or 78) now all use them. `tests/testthat/test-print-width.R` runs one fixture per census row (`helper-caution-fixtures.R`) at widths 60 and 120, with a ledger of out-of-scope lines named by kind and three guards on it. `tools/m131-caution-word-parity.R` and `tools/m131-line-identity.R` compare output with master `26bd64ac`. Three snapshot files and four pre-computed vignettes were re-rendered, and only line breaks moved. NEWS.md records the change.

**Decisions:** Prose cautions only, not tables, headers or the instrument printers. One shared helper over per-site `strwrap()` calls. Element boundaries are paragraph boundaries, as in `strwrap()`. AC4 was amended once, to byte identity against `26bd64ac` outside the census rows. The T1 census table is in git history.
