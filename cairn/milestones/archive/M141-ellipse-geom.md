# M141: A confidence-ellipse layer on the Cartesian SSM coordinates, fed from draws

**Status:** done (2026-09-20, PR #175 https://github.com/jmgirard/circumplex/pull/175)

**Goal:** Export `geom_ssm_ellipse()`, which draws a joint confidence ellipse for a profile's `(x, y)` location from a centre and a 2×2 covariance, and `ssm_ellipse_data()`, which computes those from an `ssm_draws()` object, and teach both beside the wedge in the visualization vignette.

**Outcome:** `GeomSsmEllipse` (a `GeomPath` subclass) in `R/geom_ssm.R` takes aesthetics `x0`, `y0`, `var_x`, `var_y`, `cov_xy` and parameters `level` (0.95) and `n` (100, a whole number of at least 3). `setup_data()` drops non-finite rows under the `ssm_warn_dropped()` convention, aborts naming every input row whose covariance is not positive definite, builds `n + 1` vertices from the Cholesky factor scaled by `sqrt(qchisq(level, 2))`, converts to amplitude and degrees with `d + 2*pi*(d < 0)`, unwraps along the outline with `angle_unwrap()` (so a seam ellipse crosses 360 and an origin-containing one winds once), and sets `group` to one value per retained row. `geom_ssm_ellipse()` mirrors `geom_ssm_path()` and validates `level` and `n` with `stop()`. `ssm_ellipse_data()` is a new S3 generic; its `circumplex_ssm_draws` method returns one row centred on `results$x_est`, `results$y_est` with `stats::cov()` of the `x`, `y` draws, and the default method aborts naming the class and `ssm_draws()`. Tests: a closed-form chi-square oracle over three rows, seam and origin cases with vdiffr snapshots, every abort branch, `na.rm` both ways, an input-row index after a drop, a CRAN guard in `test-plot-cran-guards.R`. The visualization vignette gained "A joint confidence ellipse from posterior draws" at the end of section 7, drawing the ellipse from the Bayesian draws fixture beside the wedge and point, with five pinned phrases in `test-vignette-latent-figures.R`. `_pkgdown.yml` rows, `GeomSsmEllipse` on the ggproto page, NEWS, and the DESIGN.md visualization list naming the path and ellipse geoms.
