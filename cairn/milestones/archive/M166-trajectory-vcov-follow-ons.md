# M166: ssm_trajectory() reads vcov by position and scales its balance check

**Status:** done (2026-10-21, PR #200 https://github.com/jmgirard/circumplex/pull/200)

**Goal:** Under the default contrast, `ssm_trajectory()` reads `vcov` through the positions of `names(coef)` and judges balance against a time scale taken from `vcov` itself.

**Outcome:** Under the default contrast, `ssm_trajectory()` reads `vcov` as `vcov[pos, pos]` with `pos <- match(needed, names(coef))`, shared by the joint-fit refusal and the balance check, so a `vcov` with no dimnames works (it failed with R's "no 'dimnames' attribute for array" error). The unbalanced-fit warning compares the spread of `t_k = -cov(intercept, slope) / var(slope)` with `sqrt(.Machine$double.eps) * sqrt(max(var(intercept) / var(slope)))`, a scale in units of time, in place of `max(1, max(abs(t_k)))`. It skips unless every slope and intercept variance is positive, and when the times or the scale overflow or the scale underflows to 0. The scale grows with the random-intercept variance relative to the residual variance, so at G x 1000 the GLS oracle's unequal-counts design passes silently; the help page and NEWS state this limit. The warning, help page and NEWS add "fixed effects beyond that model's six" as a cause (a 0/1 covariate times time on balanced data gives spread 0.086). Tests: the closed-form GLS oracle over time x 1e-6, 1, 1000 and G x 0.01, 1, 100 (unbalanced warns, balanced silent), skip cases checked against guard-deleting mutants, a positional case that a positions-2-to-7 mutant fails. No NEWS entry for the dimnames fix: the function is unreleased.

**Decisions:** none milestone-local. The plan gate chose the `sqrt(var(intercept) / var(slope))` scale over keeping the floor of 1 and over the smaller of the old and new thresholds, and reading `vcov` by position over refusing a dimnames-free `vcov` (work log, in git).
