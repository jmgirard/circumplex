# M155: The trajectory caution by input shape, and the bootstrap path into the helper

**Status:** done (2026-09-25, PR #190 https://github.com/jmgirard/circumplex/pull/190)

**Goal:** `ssm_trajectory()` prints a caution that describes its input shape, and the growth vignette's Section 7 shows the parametric bootstrap for the shipped engines and its path into the helper as `draws =`.

**Outcome:** `ssm_trajectory()` (`R/ssm_trajectory_helper.R`) sets an `input` attribute, `"coef_vcov"` or `"draws"`, on its single `structure()` return. `print.circumplex_ssm_trajectory()` branches on it. Under `coef` and `vcov` the M152 REML caution text prints unchanged and cites Section 7. Under `draws` a caution says that the intervals summarize the supplied draws as given, that their coverage depends on how the draws were produced, and that the joint fit was not checked, citing Sections 7 and 10. With no attribute (a column subset, `subset()`, a mixed stack) a caution says the shape is not recorded. A new `rbind.circumplex_ssm_trajectory()` stacks plain frames through `rbind.data.frame()`, passes `deparse.level`, `make.row.names` and `stringsAsFactors` through, drops `NULL` parts, keeps a shared `input` and removes a mixed one. R dispatches on the first argument with a method, so a plain frame later in the stack runs it too. Section 7 of `vignettes/growth-ssm-analysis.Rmd.orig` keeps master's title. It now states the REML conditioning and the M27 coverage oracle's result (glmmTMB REML, 200 persons per cell, d(t) coverage .93 to .97 certified and .854 at the uncertified wave, against this page's 150 persons, the record in `devel/` on GitHub). It states that no small-sample correction ships, and that a percentile parametric bootstrap of the fixed effects is not one on complete balanced data, because every refit's fixed effects equal OLS whatever its variance components (Zyskind's condition). It states that the helper draws from a normal on the covariance matrix alone with no t quantile, and that a draws matrix from any source enters as `draws =` with the six coefficient names and a `b_` prefix dropped. The lme4, pbkrtest and Kenward-Roger remedies are gone. Section 5, Section 10, the roxygen `@return` and details, and NEWS were rewritten to the shape-dependent caution. Tests pin the REML text byte for byte, the three branches, the caution-last rule, the draws twin's uncertified row, and the rbind cases.

**Decisions:** M155-D1: the printed caution follows the recorded input shape. Under `coef` and `vcov` the REML text prints unchanged. Under `draws` it is replaced, because the REML text described only the first shape's intervals, which D-039 reads as a correction outside GP4 while D-056's conditions hold for the first shape. No caution is removed from any shape. Reopens on a user report that the draws caution misdescribes a draws source. Plan gate: a shape-specific caution over none, a committed 1000-replicate bootstrap file (later descoped), the lme4 remedies dropped, and nlme's `simulate()` refusal routed through glmmTMB (later descoped).
