## Goal

Measure the coverage of the `ssm_trajectory()` intervals from `coef` and `vcov` at small numbers of persons, and select by a pre-registered coverage gate the small-sample draw rule that M162 ships.

## Scope

**In:** a seeded simulation oracle in `devel/` modeled on the M27 oracle, its committed results and record, the comparison of candidate draw rules, and the decision entry that names the rule. The candidate rules come from the Fable review that the plan gate asked for, plus the two rules named in AC2.

**Out:** any change to `R/`, `man/`, the vignettes or NEWS.md. The new argument, its help text, the vignette's Section 7 and the NEWS entry land in M162. Draws from a Bayesian fit (`draws =`) are not corrected; they already carry the variance-component uncertainty. A shared-intercept cell stays in the "Longitudinal deferrals" candidate row.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] AC1: `devel/m161-small-n-oracle.R` simulates replicates from the model family that the M27 oracle's header names, at waves 0 to 4. It fits each replicate by REML with the glmmTMB call that `ssm_growth_formula("glmmTMB")` returns. It runs 1000 replicates in each cell of a grid. The grid crosses the M27 oracle's `pole` and `xycor` truths with N in {10, 20, 30, 50, 200} persons. For each truth it adds missing-wave cells at N = 20 and N = 30, with 20% of person-waves deleted at random. Each cell is seeded by its level index.
- [x] AC2: The script's header fixes the candidate rules, the gated waves, the AC2 band, and the AC3 targets and tolerance. The header is in a commit that precedes the commit adding `devel/m161-small-n-results.rds`. Between those two commits, `git diff` shows no change to the header block. The candidates are three rules. R0 is the shipped normal draws. R1 is multivariate t draws with one chi-square divisor shared by all coefficients and nu = N - 1. R3a is normal draws on `vcov` multiplied by N / (N - 1). All five waves are gated for d(t), because the true a(t) is at least .59 at every wave of both truths. Band coverage counts every replicate. A failed fit, a non-positive-definite fit or a non-finite `vcov` counts as not covering. The band has two conditions, each in every complete-data cell with N in {20, 30, 50}. First, the mean 95% interval coverage over waves lies in [.93, .97] for each of e, x, y and gated d. Second, no single wave of these falls below .91. A rule that meets both passes the band.
- [x] AC3: The closed-form targets apply at wave 2 in complete-data cells. Their coverage is taken among converged fits with a positive-definite Hessian and a finite `vcov`. Under R0, the closed-form value c is P(|T| < qnorm(.975)) with T distributed as t on N - 1 df. Under R1, c is .95. The first target holds in every cell with N in {20, 30, 50, 200}. There, the 95% interval coverage of e, x and y under R0 and R1 lies within 3.5 standard errors of c. That standard error is sqrt(c (1 - c) / n), with n the count of those fits. The second target holds for each N in {20, 30, 50}. There, the R1 minus R0 coverage difference lies within 3.5 standard errors of .95 minus the R0 value of c. The difference is taken per replicate, averaged over e, x and y, and pooled over both truths. Its standard error is the standard deviation of the replicate differences divided by the square root of their count.
- [x] AC4: Two files hold the record, `devel/m161-small-n-results.rds` and `devel/m161-small-n-oracle.md`. They report the coverage of the e, x, y, a and d intervals for every rule, cell and wave. They also report the certification rate and the Monte Carlo standard error of each coverage. In each cell they report three disjoint failure counts, assigned in the order failed fit, non-positive-definite fit, non-finite `vcov`. For every complete-data cell they report the coverage among converged, positive-definite, finite-`vcov` fits, with its n, beside the coverage over all replicates. They report the paired coverage difference between each pair of rules for each cell, wave and outcome. Each difference is given on both bases, all replicates and converged fits, with its paired standard error. The N = 10 cells and the missing-wave cells are reported without a gate. The record states which rules passed the AC2 band and the AC3 values beside their targets.
- [x] AC5: In both complete-data N = 200 cells, the shipped rule's mean d(t) coverage over the gated waves lies in [.93, .97].
- [x] AC6: A D-entry in `cairn/DECISIONS.md` cites the record and states whether R1 passed the AC2 band. If R1 passes, the entry names R1 as the rule that M162 ships. Its one numeric parameter is its degrees of freedom nu, with lower bound 1. The entry states the derivation that selects R1 and cites RR25 section 1 for it. The derivation has four conditions. The model is the one `ssm_growth_formula()` returns, with random intercepts and a single linear time term. The data are complete and balanced. No person-level covariate enters. The REML estimate of the random-effect covariance is interior. Under them, the pivot at the mean design time is exactly t on N - 1 df. This holds for every linear combination of e, x and y. So nu = N - 1 there. At other times R1 is conservative for linear combinations, and for d(t) to first order. If R1 fails the band, the entry names the rules that passed the band and names no rule for M162 to ship.

## Decisions

- M161-D1 (2026-10-18, RR25 §1, §2, §6): the premise holds but is smaller than assumed. On complete balanced data REML equals the ANOVA fit, and at the mean design time (wave 2) the pivot of `e`, `x`, `y` and every linear combination is exactly t with N - 1 df under every truth. The shipped normal draws cover .918, .935, .940 and .944 at N = 10, 20, 30 and 50 there, and slightly more at the outer waves. AC2's band as planned does not separate them from a corrected rule at N = 20 to 50, and AC5's width criterion would choose a `vcov` inflation by N / (N - 1), which undercorrects (.931 at N = 10, centre wave).
- M161-D2 (2026-10-18, RR25 §3, §4): the candidate set is R0 (shipped normal draws), R1 (multivariate t, one chi-square divisor shared by all coefficients, nu = N - 1) and R3a (normal draws on `vcov` times N / (N - 1)) as a plausible-but-wrong control. Not gated: independent t marginals (no closed form; correlation attenuated .931 at nu = 9), level-specific or variance-matched inflations, design-based df rules (need T as well as N; at most 3.4% narrower than R1 at N = 20), and nu = N - 2 or N - 3 (overcorrect). No rule's df are computable from `coef` and `vcov` alone, so the opt-in argument stands.
- M161-D3 (2026-10-18, RR25 §5, §8): glmmTMB's REML `vcov` includes a variance-component term that vanishes on complete balanced data and adds 0.01% to 0.42% to the SE on 20%-missing cells, so the missing-wave cells are read like the complete ones. At N = 10 the fit reaches the boundary of the 3 by 3 random-effect covariance in 14% to 34% of replicates, so N = 10 coverage counting failures as misses reads .59 to .75 for every rule. `vcov.glmmTMB` can return an all-`NA` matrix with `pdHess` TRUE, which the oracle's `fit_joint()` must count as a failed fit.
