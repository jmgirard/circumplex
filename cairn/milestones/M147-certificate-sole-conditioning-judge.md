# M147: The certificate is the sole conditioning judge

- **Status:** in-progress
- **Priority:** normal
- **Depends on:** —
- **Driving RR:** RR24 (advisory)
- **Principles touched:** IP1, IP3, GP2, GP4
- **Resolves:** —
- **Surface tier:** user-facing — it changes which refusal literal and warning `axes_reliability()` emits on an ill-conditioned fit below the floor
- **Branch/PR:** m147-certificate-sole-conditioning-judge

## Goal

The per-fit accuracy certificate alone decides whether an `axes_reliability()` fit computes or refuses on conditioning, so the platform-dependent `"unidentified"` refusal that RR22 measured at counterexample B leaves the shipped package.

## Scope

**In:** `axes_pricing_core()` (R/axes_corrected_se.R:164) inverts the information matrix with `solve(info, tol = 0)` on the certified path. A reciprocal condition estimate below `.Machine$double.eps` no longer refuses. It selects the fit for the certificate, as the floor on `sigma` does since M111, whether or not that floor fired. The selector lives in `axes_degeneracy_refusal()` behind the M117 once-per-fit seam. A pair of bit-identical derivative matrices in `d$mats` refuses `"unidentified"` by an exact check with no tolerance, so the structural case keeps a platform-stable literal. A sweep oracle with the exact-rational driver shows the certificate under-reports at no matrix the old gate refused. The counterexample-B test admits a refusal only as an exact zero pivot, and the committed `rcond` band and its constant admission predicate are deleted. NEWS, roxygen and the refusal-vocabulary comments follow.

**Out:** the raw lavaan-tie arm of `axes_corrected_se()` keeps the default tolerance, because the certificate never prices raw Sigma-hat (D-037) and the arm is never user-reported. `solve(sigma)`'s default tolerance is unchanged; the sweep records `rcond(sigma)` so the claim that it never bites above the floor is measured, and a hit becomes a candidate row. The five remaining accuracy-certificate fragilities in DESIGN.md → M148. An `ubuntu-24.04-arm` CI job → the RR22 follow-ons candidate row. A platform survey of zero pivots at counterexample B beyond the arm64 container → not done; the two-route test shape covers it.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] AC1: `axes_pricing_core()` returns `"unidentified"` from three sites in its body and no other, which a source read of the function enumerates. The first fires on a pair of bit-identical matrices among the first `d$n_comp` matrices of `d$mats`. The second fires on one of those matrices identical to `diag(p)`. The third fires on a non-finite or exact-singular inversion of the information matrix under `solve(info, tol = 0)`. On the certified path a reciprocal condition estimate of the information matrix below `.Machine$double.eps` no longer refuses. An estimate below `sqrt(.Machine$double.eps)`, the selector threshold, routes the fit to `axes_accuracy_certificate()` through `axes_degeneracy_refusal()`, once per fit, whether the floor on `sigma` admitted the matrix or refused it `"ill_conditioned"`; `"indefinite"` and `"singular"` still return ahead of any pricing; and a routed fit computes when its certificate passes. The raw arm of `axes_corrected_se()` keeps the default tolerance, and its refusal still lands in `naive_reason`. Tests at both surfaces (`axes_corrected_se()` and `axes_scaling_factor()`) assert the identity of each ground. The duplicate-matrix ground is asserted at the one-scale construction of the M89 T10 tests and at three variants: two duplicate pairs, a non-adjacent pair, and a pair reaching the raw arm. The identity ground is asserted at the one-item-per-scale design with zeta1 fitted and at the one-item-per-block design with zeta2 fitted, at both surfaces, as `"unidentified"`. The routed ground is asserted at two floor-admitted designs with no exact dependence whose information matrix sits below the threshold: two antipodal blocks over the four cardinal scales with two items per scale and zeta2 fitted (C equals 2B minus Z up to cosine rounding), and three equally spaced scales with two items each and zeta1 fitted (C equals 1.5B minus 0.5J up to cosine rounding). Each refuses under `"uncertified"`, or under `"unidentified"` only on an exact-singular inversion, and the test asserts which identity the route it took carries. The source guard also pins the selector threshold `sqrt(.Machine$double.eps)` by name.
- [ ] AC2: A seeded sweep script `devel/degeneracy-oracle/tol0_sweep.R` enumerates its own domain in six families: the six committed certificate cases; the RR18 Q4 fixture-perturbation sweep; the M106 reachable and near-duplicate families on a kappa grid crossing the floor, plus rows at 6 and 12 equally spaced scales on the same grid and one p = 64 row at an item-error level above the floor, asserted outside the region, with the certificate's wall time recorded; a seeded random model-implied family at p in {4, 8, 9, 16, 24} including rows with a negative zeta1 or xi2; a crossed-block family fitting zeta2 (`axes_crossed_blocks()` layouts at 4 and 8 scales with 2 and 3 items, zeta2 in (0, 0.3), on the same item-error grid); and a structural family of designs singular in exact arithmetic (one item per scale with zeta1 fitted at seven item-error levels, antipodal blocks with zeta2 fitted at three). Per matrix it records the answer of the floor, `rcond(sigma)`, `rcond(info)` computed independently of the core, the number of scales, `angles_spacing_status()`, the answers of `axes_fits_zeta1()` and `axes_fits_zeta2()`, the default-tolerance inversion outcome, the `tol = 0` outcome, the shipped refusal predicate's answer and, in the region (the default tolerance refused, `tol = 0` inverted, and the core refused on no structural ground), the exact-rational oracle's true relative error and the certificate's estimate. Family membership does not decide region membership; the core's answer does, and no oracle is called outside the region. Its pre-registered acceptance is stated in the script header and holds on the committed run (`tol0-sweep-results.rds` beside it, regenerable by the script). (a) At every region matrix whose doubles priced, the certificate's estimate is not below the true relative error, so each such matrix refuses `"uncertified"` or computes with a true error inside delta_star; a region matrix whose doubles refused after the inversion refuses. Every matrix both tolerances refused, whose floor answer is neither `"indefinite"` nor `"singular"`, is refused `"unidentified"`. (b1) At every structural-family matrix `rcond(info)` is below the selector threshold divided by 1e4. (b2) Every matrix in the committed run that the floor admits, whose recorded columns show a design the exported API admits (four or more equally spaced scales, zeta1 per `axes_fits_zeta1()`, zeta2 per `axes_fits_zeta2()`), and that the selector routes, passes its certificate; the summary lists each with its estimate; the list is non-empty; at least one crossed-block matrix with `axes_fits_zeta2()` TRUE is floor-admitted in that domain; and the p = 3 rows stay measured and are reported as out of domain. (c) Every matrix inverted under the default tolerance returns bit-identical `si`, `sim` and `acov` under `tol = 0`, a property the header states holds by construction of `solve()`. (d) No family is empty, and the run contains at least one p = 64 row, at least one row each at 6 and 12 scales, and at least one row with a negative zeta1 or xi2.

## Decisions

- 2026-09-21 (RR24 Q1): the certificate is licensed below eps by mechanism, not only by measurement: both routes run one algorithm on bit-identical inputs at roundoff eps and about eps squared, so the estimate-to-truth ratio is pinned at the safety factor (9.9996 to 10.005 at the 55 graded region matrices with true error below 1e-2); an under-report needs the double-double route sixteen decades unluckier than the double route at one matrix. Unmeasured classes to sweep before T5: zeta2 designs, p = 64, negative component estimates, 6 and 12 scales.
- 2026-09-21 (RR24 Q2, Q4): selector threshold `sqrt(.Machine$double.eps)`, home `axes_degeneracy_refusal()` with the core returning `rcond(info)` as data; the certificate on every fit rejected (140x the pricing's cost, exposure of every fit to the certificate surface). Promoted to D-062.
- 2026-09-21 (RR24 Q3): the exact structural check is the pairwise clause plus an identity clause; a rank test rejected (adds a tolerance to a literal meaning bit-identity); dropping the check rejected (an exact zero pivot on identical rows is not guaranteed by blocked LU). Promoted to D-062.
- 2026-09-21 (RR24 Q5): AC2 (b) restated as the threshold by name, a low-side gap at committed structural probes, and a high-side list of routed reachable fits; "reachable" is the API's conjunction (four or more equally spaced scales, zeta1 per `axes_fits_zeta1()`, zeta2 per `axes_fits_zeta2()`), and the p = 3 rows stay measured and out of domain.
- 2026-09-21 (RR24 Q6): the raw arm keeps the default tolerance; `naive_reason` becomes the one platform-dependent literal, at counterexample B in particular, and any test asserting it in the band takes the two-route shape; AC4's prose says so.
- 2026-09-21 (re-audit of AC1): the duplicate check compares only the first `d$n_comp` matrices of `d$mats`, a narrowing of D-061's "derivative matrices" that D-062's text does not state: every component matrix has a unit diagonal, so none can equal an item-error matrix, and two item-error matrices are never identical (RR24 section 3).
- 2026-09-21 (RR24 B2): the p = 3 Q4 perturbation `t = 0.999975` is the first floor-admitted matrix measured with a certificate past the target (cval 1.67e-2); unreachable, recorded in the sweep summary and in D-062's Reopens as a tau matter at four or more scales.
