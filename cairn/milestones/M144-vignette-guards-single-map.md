## Goal

The vignette guards that remain each check shipped behavior from one level map, and the guard with no shipped job is gone.

## Scope

**In:** the level map and reading order move to `tests/testthat/helper-vignette-frame.R`, read by both the frame test and the pkgdown script. The frame test fails an Overview whose `Section N` number does not match its heading. `_pkgdown.yml` drops the hand-written Vignettes menu, and each `articles:` group carries a `navbar:` heading so pkgdown builds the grouped dropdown. `tools/check-pkgdown-vignettes.R` shrinks to a diff of `vignettes/` and the `articles:` index against the map, and runs as a step in `.github/workflows/pkgdown.yaml`. `tools/check-vignette-split.R` is deleted. The boundary test's scoping comment is reworded. NEWS names the menu change. This reverses the M138 plan's rule-out of pkgdown's `articles: navbar:` grouping, recorded as a milestone-local decision at T4.

**Out:** the three frame-test false-failure shapes (a Level line wrapped over two source lines, a curly-quoted title, a References heading not last) are pruned: each fails with a message today and no page uses those forms. The split guard's own five findings (advisory word cap, empty added region, a second ` => `, chunk-comment blanking, and the boundary comment's overstatement, the last fixed here) go with the deletion. Hardening the hand-written menu's guard (divider count and placement, an empty heading, a group title outside the map, a page above the first heading, the dash-plus-href message) is moot once the menu is generated. Running the width guard's exemption fixes stays on its own `[high]` candidate row. The Cartesian canvas follow-ons stay on their row.
