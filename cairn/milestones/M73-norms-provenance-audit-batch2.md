## Goal

Verify the shipped norms of the four single-norm-sample instruments (iis32,
iis64, ipipipc, isc) against their published sources, and repair the four M72
audit defects that need no multi-sample support.

## Scope

**In:** source notes for `hatcher2012` (iis32), `hatcher2009` (iis64),
`markey2009` (ipipipc) and `hopwood2011` (isc), each with an audit-values block;
extending `AUDIT_BATCH` in `data-raw/audit-norms.R` to nine instruments and
dispositioning every ledger row; correcting any shipped value the audit finds
wrong; the anchored-`Reference` rule that closes M72's two review findings about
the `Reference` comparison (containment admits a contradicting string; the
note-side value is an authored credit, so both sides share an origin); the
zero-row ledger stamping crash; binding the test's pin list to
`norms-audit.md`'s verdicts in both directions; correcting the stale "(in
press)" `@source` credits for iitc and iei.

**Out:** the multi-sample join key and per-sample citekeys, and the four
multi-sample instruments (cais, iei, igicr, iipsc) → M74. iip32/iip64 → a
ROADMAP candidate row, promoted when the IIP manual reaches the shelf. The
csig PA/NO duplicated-statistics question → its existing candidate row, awaiting
the source author.

Returned to `in-progress` on two criteria.

- **AC6 — defect return.** F4 is a one-line fix: iis32's verdict must name
  `Reference` and `URL`. F3 rides with it (the same line's `Population`
  clause), as do F10, F11, F12 and F18, which are accuracy defects in
  artifacts this branch authored.
- **AC5 — amendment return.** The criterion asks for "the page or line at
  which the source prints that credit". No journal article prints an
  author-year short form as a string, so seven of nine sources cannot satisfy
  it however well the work is done; locke2007 passes only because its source

  rather than the work — its purpose, giving the note-side credit an origin
  independent of the shipped string, is met by anchoring the author names and
  year to the page that prints them. This routes to a gated amendment.
