# M170: Measure the CPM engine's missed optima and settle which solution it reports

- **Status:** review

## Goal

Measure how often and by how much a lower discrepancy than `cpm_engine()`'s estimate exists, and compare the engine's estimate with two alternatives, so that a decision rule fixed before the run settles which solution the estimate should be.

## Scope

**In:** a seeded study `devel/m170-engine-estimate.R` (helpers beside it) with a pre-registration block, a timing pilot, a full run, a record `devel/m170-engine-estimate.md` and a results file `devel/m170-engine-estimate-results.rds`. A Fable review of the pre-registration before the full run. Four real or published fits. A D-entry with the decision, and DESIGN.md's D-071 section pointing at it.

Definitions. E is `cpm_engine()` as shipped. W is E plus a fixed deterministic set of extra starts: around E's estimate, a communality-wall start per scale, an all-communality-level start, starts with each free angle moved by 90, -90 and 180 degrees, pairwise swaps of two free angles, and each swap with the wall on one swapped scale; around the theory start, a communality-wall start per scale (RR28). W keeps the lowest discrepancy. H is the lowest discrepancy over W's starts plus 2K seeded random starts around the theory start at sd .7 and 2K at sd 1.2 (RR28). H_K uses only the first K starts of each family. W and H run the same boundary polish as E. Cells: M167's two truths (boundary, interior; p = 8, communality .75, m = 3, variant A, unit scaling) at raw-data N = 250, 500, 1000 and correlation-matrix N = 2000, 5000, with M167's data generators and fresh seeds.

**Out:**
- Shipping any change to `cpm_engine()`, `cpm_fit()`, `print()`, `summary()` or the bootstrap goes to the ship-step candidate row that AC6 writes, when the decision changes behavior. That row also carries the formal T calibration re-run for the shipped estimator, the CIRCUM/CircE oracle re-check, the bootstrap warm-start change, and bootstrap coverage of an adopted alternative. "Catch errors in CPM bootstrap refits" joins it if that step opens `cpm_bootstrap()`.
- The help-page statement is M169.
- Non-normal data, other truths and instruments, variants B to D, and the free scaling family stay outside the measured envelope. The D-entry's scope names them, as D-068 did.
- Profile-likelihood intervals stay in the "Boundary-regime interval improvement" row.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] AC1: A pre-registration block at the top of `devel/m170-engine-estimate.R` fixes these items: the truths, cells and seeds, including the seeds of the AC5 fits; the replicate count rule from the timing pilot; the definitions of E, W and H, with K; the threshold for a lower discrepancy (0.01 in T units, T = (N - 1) times the discrepancy); the saturation rule; every metric that AC3 and AC4 name; and the decision rule. The saturation rule compares H_K with H, and compares H with an independent search family (another spread or centre, fixed in the block) on a stated subsample per cell. The decision rule is an ordered if/else chain over named metrics. It ends in a default branch and selects one of five options: keep E unchanged; keep E and add a diagnostic for a lower optimum outside the theoretical configuration; report W; report H; or report E with the lowest discrepancy found beside it. It selects W or H only where the saturation verdict passes in the cells it relies on and AC4's figures favor that estimator. Otherwise it selects among the three options that keep E. The block states how it breaks a tie between the diagnostic and the report-beside option under GP4. (RB tripwire: no-oracle)
- [x] AC2: For each cell, `devel/m170-engine-estimate.md` reports the share of accepted E fits where H_K and where H find a discrepancy lower than E's by more than the threshold. The figures are computed from `devel/m170-engine-estimate-results.rds`. The record also reports the saturation rule's verdict. In a cell whose verdict is a fail, H's shares are labelled as lower bounds in the record and in the D-entry. H's AC4 figures are labelled everywhere as describing H, not the global minimum.
- [x] AC3: For each cell and for each of W and H, the record reports these figures from the same results file, over accepted E fits. (a) The share with a discrepancy lower than E's by more than the threshold. (b) The median and largest gap in T units among those fits. (c) The share of those whose lower point has more scales with a communality above .995 than E's. (d) The share of those whose cyclic angle order, after `cpm_canonicalize()`, differs from E's. (e) W's median CPU time per fit on an unloaded core, beside E's.
- [x] AC4: For each cell and for each of E, W and H, the record reports these figures from the same results file, over accepted E fits. (a) The bias and RMSE of each free angle (the signed circular difference from the truth), each communality and each weight. (b) Per parameter, the share of fits with a defined analytic standard error, and the 95% Wald coverage on those fits. (c) The share of fits whose T exceeds the .95 chi-square quantile at the degrees of freedom of the model as fitted at that point.
- [x] AC5: The record reports four real or published fits, each at the settings its oracle test or vignette uses (m, angles and model). The fits are: `jz2017`'s eight octant scales on the raw-data path; the matrices from `cpm_oracle_voc()` and `cpm_oracle_verbal()` in `tests/testthat/helper-cpm-oracles.R`; and the simulated `sim` sample that the `cpm-boundary-fits` vignette fits as `demo`. For each, the record gives E's T and estimates. For W and H it gives the gap in T units. Where the gap exceeds the threshold, it also gives their estimates and any change in angle order.

## Decisions

- M170-D1 (2026-10-27, RR28): the study runs, amended (RR28 Q8). Not running would leave the question on RR27's uncommitted survey, with no detection rate for any deterministic probe, so no diagnostic could be specified. The amended study costs the same 12 CPU-hours.
- M170-D2 (2026-10-27, RR28): RR28's answers as applied. W gets 101 deterministic starts, adding swaps, swaps with the wall, and wall starts around g0, because the interior-truth lower points are two-scale swaps that single-angle shifts cannot reach (Q1). H is a bounded reference search, never the global minimum. Its random starts are both around g0 (Q2, rec 13). Saturation is expected to fail at N <= 1000 and at interior N = 2000, which leaves branches 3 to 6 (Q3). favors() splits into no_worse in every cell and improves in at least one (Q4). Branch 5 reads a 95% lower bound on W's median gap (Q5, Q7). The default stays the diagnostic, and the tie-break names IP3 beside GP4 (Q5). "Report H" means a later search shown to reach the same point whatever its seed (Q6). W's detection power is reported beside any diagnostic (Q6).
- M170-D3 (2026-10-27, RR28): not taken. Rec 14 would drop the saturation requirement from branch 1 for W. It disagrees with AC1's wording, its practical effect is nil because favors(W) is expected to fail on its own, and amending a criterion for no effect is not worth the amendment. Rec 15 would apply one Wilson allowance to every threshold, but the share thresholds are read far from their bars (RR28 Q5, Q7), so point estimates stay. Rejections accepted: a "keep E" default (16), removing branch 2 (17), more all-communality levels (18), not running the study (19), raising K to chase saturation (20).
